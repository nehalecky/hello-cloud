💡 Recommended: Foundation Horizon

Based on your emphasis on developer tooling, practicality, and time-to-value combined with state-of-the-art models and innovation, Foundation Horizon balances trust (blue) with energy (orange) perfectly. The "horizon" metaphor captures forecasting beautifully, while the contrast creates an authentic identity distinct from FastAPI's teal or Pydantic's pink.

How to Choose

1. Visual Impact: Which palette immediately catches your eye?
2. Brand Message: Which colors best represent "state-of-the-art forecasting made practical"?
3. Differentiation: Which stands out from competitors while remaining professional?
4. Longevity: Which colors will you still love in 6 months? 2 years?
5. Cloud Art Synergy: Which works best with your subtle cloud algorithmic art?
